What go-autodiff will cover

automatic differentiation, in pure Go.

go-autodiff is the planned home of automatic differentiation for go-scikit: forward and reverse modes, with Jacobian and Hessian helpers that feed go-numerics/optimize gradients and the JacFunc of go-numerics/integrate, both of which take user-supplied derivatives or finite differences today. It is in wave 2 (PLAN.md item 8). Everything below is planned, not released.

→

Forward mode

Dual and hyper-dual numbers: one sweep per Jacobian-vector product, simple and exact to rounding.

←

Reverse mode

A tape (Wengert list) recorded by calling tape methods: one sweep per gradient.

∇

Jacobians and Hessians

Helpers for Jacobians and Hessians, with forward-over-reverse Hessian-vector products.

⚙

Feeds the solvers

Gradients for optimize, and an AD-built Jacobian for the stiff solvers' JacFunc in integrate.

🧪

Checked three ways

Against analytic derivatives, against finite differences (CheckGrad), and against JAX as an oracle.

🧭

Honest about Go

Go has no operator overloading, so user code is written against an AD type; the design weighs duals, a tape, source transformation and generics.

📦

Pure Go, CGO=0

No cgo, the same results on every architecture the fleet tests.

⚖️

BSD-3-Clause

GPL implementations (pbenner/autodiff) are read-only; Tapenade, Enzyme and JAX are design references and oracles.
